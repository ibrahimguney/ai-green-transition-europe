# Journal Targeting Shortlist

_Last reviewed: August 2026. Quartiles can change annually. The Q1 labels below refer to currently visible SCImago/Scopus-based rankings; the current Clarivate JCR quartile should be rechecked immediately before submission._

## Recommended submission sequence

### 1. Journal of Cleaner Production — preferred first target

**Why it fits:** The journal explicitly covers cleaner production, energy/resource efficiency, environmental assessment, corporate sustainability, and sustainability policy. The paper's core contribution—testing whether enterprise AI diffusion is already associated with lower sector-level GHG intensity—fits its digitalisation-and-cleaner-production agenda. Recent JCP papers also examine AI together with sustainability and cleaner-energy outcomes.

**Current open-ranking signal:** SCImago Q1.

**What to strengthen before submission:**
- foreground the cleaner-production mechanism rather than AI technology alone;
- emphasise sector-specific production structure and renewable-energy context;
- add a concise policy/managerial implications subsection;
- include a graphical conceptual framework linking AI adoption, efficiency, computing demand, rebound, and GHG intensity.

**Risk:** A null AI coefficient must be framed as a theoretically informative boundary condition, not as a purely negative result.

### 2. Sustainable Production and Consumption — very strong fit

**Why it fits:** The study is directly concerned with whether digital technology improves the environmental efficiency of production. The journal covers environmental engineering, industrial/manufacturing systems, sustainable production, and renewable-energy/sustainability topics.

**Current open-ranking signal:** SCImago Q1. The publisher page currently displays a CiteScore of 22.5 and Impact Factor of 9.6.

**What to strengthen before submission:**
- present NACE-sector heterogeneity as a sustainable-production finding;
- make the distinction between adoption and operational AI intensity explicit;
- add a figure comparing grouped SHAP importance across structural and digital predictors.

### 3. Journal of Environmental Management — strong topical alternative

**Why it fits:** The journal focuses on environmental management, monitoring, policy, pollution, and sustainability. A closely related 2025 study on AI intensity and digital rebound was published in this journal, demonstrating clear topical relevance.

**Current open-ranking signal:** SCImago Q1. The publisher page currently displays a CiteScore of 14.4 and Impact Factor of 8.4.

**What to strengthen before submission:**
- expand the environmental-governance implications;
- connect the France/Sweden series-break robustness and measurement limitations to monitoring policy;
- frame the paper as evidence against assuming automatic environmental benefits from digitalisation.

### 4. Energy Economics — ambitious target

**Why it fits:** The journal explicitly covers energy use, environment/climate, forecasting, and rigorous econometric analysis. A 2025 article on nonlinear AI–carbon relationships and clean energy was published here.

**Current open-ranking signal:** SCImago Q1. The publisher page currently displays a CiteScore of 21.7 and Impact Factor of 14.2.

**Main challenge:** The journal describes itself as a premier energy-economics field journal and expects rigorous, replicable economic analysis. The current three-wave adoption panel and associational FE design may be considered too short unless the theoretical mechanism and identification are strengthened.

**Submission only after:**
- stronger economic-theory framing;
- additional sector/energy heterogeneity tests;
- preferably a longer AI series or an identification extension.

### 5. Technological Forecasting and Social Change — ambitious technology-focused target

**Why it fits:** The journal studies technological innovation and its societal/environmental consequences. Enterprise AI diffusion and green-transition outcomes are clearly within the broad theme.

**Current open-ranking signal:** SCImago Q1. The publisher page currently displays a CiteScore of 26.3 and Impact Factor of 13.3.

**Main challenge:** The journal explicitly requires substantial theoretical and practical contributions and warns against manuscripts without strong current theory. The present manuscript would need a more developed theoretical framework around general-purpose technology diffusion, complementary assets, and rebound mechanisms.

## Practical recommendation

For the manuscript in its current empirical form, the recommended route is:

**Journal of Cleaner Production → Sustainable Production and Consumption → Journal of Environmental Management**

Energy Economics and Technological Forecasting and Social Change should be treated as higher-risk targets unless the theoretical and identification contributions are strengthened.

## Before choosing the final journal

Recheck:
1. current JCR and Scimago quartiles;
2. current aims and scope;
3. word limit and reference format;
4. open-access/APC requirements;
5. generative-AI disclosure policy;
6. data/code availability requirements;
7. whether graphical abstract and highlights are required.

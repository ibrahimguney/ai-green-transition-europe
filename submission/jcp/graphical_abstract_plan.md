# Graphical Abstract Plan

## Core message

**AI adoption alone is not a robust short-run predictor of cleaner production; sector and energy-economic context dominate.**

## Recommended left-to-right visual flow

### Panel 1 — Data linkage

**Enterprise AI adoption**
Eurostat ICT survey

↓ exact country × NACE × year matching

**GHG intensity**
Eurostat air-emission accounts

### Panel 2 — Analytical design

**Econometrics**
- two-way fixed effects

**Explainable ML**
- XGBoost
- country-grouped validation
- grouped SHAP

### Panel 3 — Main findings

**Fixed effects**
- AI → GHG intensity: not statistically robust
- U-shaped digital rebound: not supported

**Prediction**
- out-of-country R² = 0.87

**Grouped SHAP ranking**
1. NACE sector
2. Real GDP per capita
3. Renewable-energy share
4. R&D intensity
5. AI adoption
6. Year

### Panel 4 — Take-home implication

**Cleaner production does not follow automatically from AI diffusion.**

Sectoral production structure and clean-energy context matter more in the observable short run.

## Design guidance

- Use a horizontal, left-to-right composition.
- Keep text minimal; prefer icons for AI, industry, emissions, energy, and model analysis.
- Avoid decorative AI imagery that implies causal environmental benefits.
- Make the null/weak AI finding visually neutral rather than negative or alarming.
- Use one clear visual hierarchy: Data → Methods → Evidence → Implication.

Prepare the final graphical abstract as a separate image using Elsevier's general graphical-abstract guidance: minimum 1328 × 531 pixels (or the same aspect ratio) and at least 300 dpi.

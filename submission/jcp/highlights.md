# Highlights

- Enterprise AI adoption shows no robust short-run reduction in GHG intensity.
- A U-shaped digital rebound effect is not supported in the European panel.
- Sector structure dominates AI adoption in grouped SHAP importance.
- GDP and renewable-energy share outperform AI in predicting GHG intensity.
- XGBoost reaches R²=0.87 under country-grouped cross-validation.

## Core positioning

The manuscript should not claim that AI has no environmental effect. The evidence supports the narrower statement that, in the currently observable three-wave European country-sector panel, **enterprise AI adoption does not show a robust independent association with lower GHG intensity and does not support the pre-specified U-shaped digital rebound effect**.

This is contrasted with recent long-horizon global studies that use AI-development or AI-intensity proxies and report emission-reducing, inverted-U, threshold, or rebound effects.

## Contribution to emphasize

1. Direct enterprise AI adoption measure.
2. Country × NACE × year design.
3. Official Eurostat GHG intensity outcome.
4. Exact NACE harmonisation.
5. Country-sector fixed effects plus country-clustered inference.
6. Out-of-country XGBoost validation.
7. Grouped SHAP at conceptual-feature level.
